Services

Three ways we take manual work off your team’s plate.

OCR trained on your forms

We don’t point a generic OCR engine at your documents and hope. We train the recognition system directly on your business’s paperwork and handwriting until it beats off-the-shelf accuracy on the forms you actually use — so data entry clerks stop retyping what the system already read correctly.

  • Learns from every human correction
  • Confidence-tiered review queue — only true exceptions reach a person
  • Full audit trail from scanned image to system record

Digital forms, hosted for you

We rebuild your paper forms as clean, mobile-friendly digital forms and host the whole thing. Your team fills them out on a phone, tablet, or PC — even offline — and submissions flow straight into your systems. No printing, no scanning, no re-keying.

  • Works offline in the field, syncs when back online
  • Same underlying data model as your OCR pipeline
  • One hosted platform your team always reaches at the same link

Agentic AI consulting & development

Beyond documents, we design and build agentic AI systems that plug into the tools you already run — automating multi-step tasks, approvals, and internal processes, with oversight and guardrails built in from day one, not bolted on after.

  • Scoped to your real workflows, not a generic chatbot
  • Integrates with your existing software and data
  • Human oversight and logging built into every agent
