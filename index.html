The engine underneath

ZeroType doesn’t call a vendor and hope. It reads forms the way your team does.

Azure, Google and Amazon can transcribe a crop of text — that’s genuinely what they’re good at. None of them know what your blank form looks like, what your team’s handwriting looks like, or what your business calls things. That’s the work ZeroType does first. The vendor model sits behind our own engine as a fallback for the rare word nobody here has confirmed yet — it is not the thing doing the reading.

Off-the-shelf OCR API

  • Reads the whole page, ink and printed form together, every time
  • Bills the vendor for every field on every page, forever
  • Guesses at your site names, part numbers and shorthand from a generic dictionary
  • No memory between documents — page 500 is read exactly as blindly as page 1
  • A low-confidence guess often comes back looking like a clean answer

ZeroType

  • Registers each page against its own blank template and subtracts the printed form, so only what your team wrote is left to read
  • Checks new writing against your own confirmed examples first; the vendor is only asked about what that corpus hasn’t seen
  • Builds a living dictionary of your business’s own vocabulary from every correction your team makes
  • Gets more accurate and cheaper to run the longer you use it — a correction on document one pays off on document two
  • A value it isn’t sure of is never guessed — it’s flagged for one person to confirm, one field, not one document

Blank-form learning

Every page is registered against the exact blank it came from, then the printed lines, boxes and letterhead are digitally subtracted out — leaving only the ink your team put down for the engine to read.

Preprocessing most engines skip

Faded carbon copies, curled paper, uneven lighting, skewed phone photos. Pages are measured, straightened and repaired before a single character is read — without inventing ink that was never there, and without billing a vendor to read a page that was never going to come back clean.

Your business’s own language

Site names, internal codes, abbreviations, industry shorthand — the words no generic model has ever seen. ZeroType builds that vocabulary from your own corrections instead of guessing off a public dictionary.

Nothing is invented

If a field can’t be read with real confidence, it goes to a person instead of getting guessed at — one field to confirm, never a whole document to redo, with every decision kept on the record.
